KNOW YOUR SKIN

Four common skin types

Understanding your skin type can help you choose suitable skincare products.

  • Dry Skin: Feels tight or flaky, needs hyaluronic acid and ceramides.
  • Oily Skin: Has more shine, benefits from salicylic acid and niacinamide.
  • Combination Skin: Has an oily T-zone and normal/dry cheeks.
  • Sensitive Skin: Easily reacts with redness, prefers fragrance-free products.

SKINCARE BASICS

Basic Daily Routine Steps

Skincare does not have to be complicated. Follow these simple steps daily:

  1. Cleansing: Cleanse your skin with a gentle formula every morning and night.
  2. Hydration: Apply a suitable moisturizer according to your skin needs.
  3. Protection: Use sun protection (SPF) during the daytime.
